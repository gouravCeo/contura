Why Most Indian Bras Fail Heavy Busts

The average Indian bra design prioritizes aesthetics over engineering. Thin straps, shallow cups, and weak bands are common — all disastrous for heavy busts. A full cup bra with a firm band should carry 80% of the weight, not your shoulders.

Best Bra Styles for Heavy Busts

  • Full Cup Bra: Maximum coverage. Essential for D cup and above.
  • Side-Support Balconette: Pushes tissue forward, creating a narrower silhouette.
  • Minimizer: Reduces projection by redistributing tissue. Great under kurtis.
  • Wire-Free Support: Wide straps and firm underband for all-day comfort.
