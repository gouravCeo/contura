Zivame vs Clovia: Bra Sizing Compared

By Sarah J. • May 08, 2026 • 5 min read

Two giants dominate India's online lingerie market: Zivame and Clovia. But their sizing systems are not identical. If you've ever ordered the same size from both and gotten completely different fits, this guide explains why.

The Short Answer

Clovia runs slightly smaller in the band and deeper in the cup. Zivame is truer to international US/UK sizing but has shallower cups. If you're between sizes, go up a band in Clovia or down a cup in Zivame.
